How we purify

Five stages to
absolute purity

Every drop of NectarLush water passes through five scientifically validated purification stages before it reaches your hands — no shortcuts, no compromises.

01
Sand Filtration
Removes suspended solids, sediment, and large particulates from raw water.
02
Micron Filtration
Fine membrane filters eliminate microscopic particles and fine sediment.
03
Reverse Osmosis
RO membranes remove dissolved salts, heavy metals, and chemical impurities.
04
UV Treatment
Ultraviolet light neutralises bacteria, viruses, and all biological contaminants.
05
Ozonization
Ozone treatment provides a final disinfection layer and enhances shelf stability.

"After five stages of rigorous purification, what reaches you is not just water — it is trust, sealed fresh."
