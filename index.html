Our story

Born from a belief
in pure water

Nectar Lush Beverages was founded on the conviction that access to truly pure, well-packaged water should be within reach of every home and business in India.

Purity Without Compromise
Every batch undergoes rigorous multi-stage testing before it leaves our facility. We publish our water quality reports openly.
Responsible Manufacturing
Our plant is designed to minimise water waste and energy consumption. We recover and recycle process water at every stage of production.
Community Roots
